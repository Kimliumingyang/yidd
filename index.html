We're YiDD Studio, an independent creative game studio based in Auckland, New Zealand.

We love bold characters, energetic visuals, unusual ideas, and the kind of game you open for five minutes and end up playing much longer.

Our work explores browser-friendly experiences, from arcade shooters and puzzles to wonderfully silly experiments. We design worlds that feel handmade, approachable, and full of personality.
